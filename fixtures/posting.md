# Senior Data Engineer, Brightwell Analytics (fictional posting, for tests and demos)

Location: Amsterdam, Netherlands (hybrid, 2 days on site)
Reference: BW-DE-0417

Brightwell Analytics builds demand-forecasting software for grocery chains. Our data platform team
owns the pipelines that feed every forecast, and we are hiring a senior engineer to grow it.

## What you will do
- Own batch pipelines that move retail sales data into our warehouse every night
- Raise the quality bar: tests, monitoring and clear ownership for every table
- Work with data scientists to get their features into production
- Mentor engineers and lead design reviews

## Requirements
- 5+ years building production data pipelines in **Python** and **SQL**
- Hands-on experience orchestrating workflows with **Airflow**
- Experience with **dbt** for warehouse modelling
- Data-quality testing in practice (we use **Great Expectations**)
- Working knowledge of **Scala** and **Spark** for our heavy transformations

## Nice to have
- Streaming experience with **Kafka**
- Mentoring or tech-lead experience

Send a CV and a one-page cover letter.
