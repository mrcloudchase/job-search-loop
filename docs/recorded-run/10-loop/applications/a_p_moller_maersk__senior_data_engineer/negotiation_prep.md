## Negotiation points

1. **On-call and how it is compensated.** This bears directly on a stated deal-breaker ("on-call without compensation"). Ask for written terms: whether an on-call rota exists, how often you would be on call, and the remuneration (extra pay, time off in lieu or allowance). Ask that any on-call be either absent from the role or compensated explicitly.
2. **Weekly hours.** This is a stated requirement ("at most 40 hours per week") and the offer does not state hours. Ask for the contractual weekly hours to be confirmed in writing as 40, together with how the "3 days in the office" applies.
3. **Base pay.** The offer states DKK 720,000 per year. Having looked up the market level for the role, ask for a base at or above what you have established as appropriate, and note that with no equity, base pay and the bonus carry the whole value of the package.
4. **Bonus terms.** The offer says "up to 10 percent of base, paid yearly" without criteria. Ask for the conditions, the target versus maximum, and how it has been paid recently; and ask whether the maximum can be raised, since there is no equity to add upside.
5. **Level, team and role scope.** This bears on the stated want of being a senior individual contributor or tech lead on a data platform team and on the career goal of a tech lead role within 3 years. Ask for the team, reporting line and level to be confirmed in the offer or contract, and for a written statement of the progression path and the criteria for moving to tech lead.
6. **Relocation support.** The offer says nothing about moving from the Netherlands. Ask for relocation assistance (moving costs, travel, a settling-in allowance or housing support) or, failing that, a suitable allowance, given that the role is in Copenhagen and you currently live in the Netherlands.
7. **Commuting and office location.** With a stated constraint of at most one hour each way, ask which office the role is based in and whether there is flexibility on which days you are on site in case the commute is long.
8. **Contract terms, start date, pension and holiday details.** The offer does not state whether the contract is permanent, the probation period or the holiday-pay arrangement, and it states "pension 12 percent" and "6 weeks holiday" without detail. Ask for these to be clarified and confirmed in writing: whether the pension is employer-funded in full and how holiday time and holiday pay are arranged. The offer meets your start date of 2027-02-01 and accepts your three months notice, so ask that these be confirmed in writing so the alignment is protected.
