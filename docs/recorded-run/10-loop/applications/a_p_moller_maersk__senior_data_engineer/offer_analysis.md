- "more than 4 days a week on site; on-call without compensation" — **on-site part meets, on-call part does not say.** The offer states hybrid, 3 days in the office, which is on site on no more than 4 days a week. The offer says nothing about on-call or its compensation, so the on-call part of this deal-breaker is unconfirmed; the posting mentions "incident support", which leaves the question open.

Wants:

- "a senior individual-contributor or tech-lead role on a data platform team" — **partly meets, partly does not say.** The title is Senior Data Engineer, consistent with a senior individual-contributor role, and the posting describes "platform engineering" and data platforms. The offer does not state the team, whether the role is individual contributor or tech lead, or that it sits on a data platform team; the posting includes "incident support".

Other stated targets relevant to the offer:

- "Career goals: tech lead within 3 years" — **does not say** (the offer states nothing about career progression).
- "Work authorisation: may work anywhere in the EU without sponsorship" — **no conflict stated**: the offer does not mention sponsorship. Whether sponsorship is needed is not stated as an issue.
- "Security clearance: none" — **does not say**: the offer and posting do not state any clearance requirement.
- "Employment status: employed, serving a three months notice period if I leave" — **meets** (the offer says they accept the three months notice).
- "Hours: full-time, at most 40 hours per week" — **does not say** (the offer states no weekly hours).
- "Start date: three months notice, earliest start 2027-02-01" — **meets** (the offer's start date is 2027-02-01, and it says they accept the three months notice).
- "Ideal locations: Copenhagen" — **meets** (the offer is for Copenhagen).
- "Constraints: at most one hour of commuting each way" — **does not say** (the offer names no office or address, so commute time cannot be assessed; the posting says "Copenhagen Metropolitan Area").

## Gaps

Against the targets:

- The offer says nothing about on-call, which is part of a stated deal-breaker, and the posting's "incident support" and "Operational Excellence" language makes it a live question rather than an unlikely one.
- Weekly hours are not stated, so the "at most 40 hours per week" target is unconfirmed.
- The role's level and team are not stated in the offer, so whether it is a senior individual contributor or a tech lead on a data platform team is open.
- Nothing on progression toward tech lead within 3 years.
- No office address, so the one-hour-each-way commute limit cannot be checked.
- No information on relocation from the Netherlands, or on any support for it.
- Equity is explicitly "none".
- The offer is thin on the full pay picture: bonus conditions, probation, contract type and holiday-pay arrangement are all unstated.

Against the posting:

- **Individual contributor vs. lead.** The posting describes a role that works "across data engineering, analytics enablement, visualization, and AI/ML engineering", collaborates with Product, Analytics, Platform, Data Science and AI teams, supports business stakeholders, and includes "incident support". The candidate's wish for an individual-contributor or tech-lead role on a data platform team is not contradicted, but the breadth suggests a generalist senior role rather than a narrow platform IC.
- **Incident support.** The posting lists "Experience with DevOps and DataOps practices, including CI/CD, monitoring, observability, and incident support" under required experience, and has "Operational Excellence" as a themed section. The offer says nothing about how such work is scheduled or compensated.
- **In-person days.** The posting does not state a working pattern; the offer's "3 days in the office" applies.
- **Location.** The posting says "Copenhagen Metropolitan Area", and the offer says "Copenhagen". No office site is identified.
- **No salary or compensation detail in the posting** to compare against the offer. The posting also does not mention relocation, clearance, probation or hours.

## Questions to ask

- Is there any on-call or out-of-hours support rota in this role? How often, what is the response expectation, and how is it compensated (extra pay, time off in lieu, allowance)?
- How many hours per week is the role expected to be? Is it a standard 40-hour week, and is there regular overtime?
- Which team will I join, and is the role a senior individual contributor, a tech lead, or something in between?
- What is the progression path from this role toward tech lead, and what does the timeline look like in practice?
- Which office will I work from, and what is its address (so I can assess commute time)?
- Are relocation support or allowances available for moving from the Netherlands (including any assistance with housing, travel or moving costs)?
- What are the exact bonus conditions — on what is the "up to 10 percent" based, who decides, and what has been paid in recent years?
- How do holiday time and holiday pay work around the "6 weeks holiday" and "pension 12 percent"? For example, are there paid public holidays, and is there a holiday allowance in addition?
- Is the contract permanent or fixed-term? Is there a probation period, and if so how long and on what terms?
- Does the pension contribution come wholly from the employer, or is any of it deducted from my pay?
- Is there a signing bonus or any other one-off payment?
- Does the role involve security clearance or background checks beyond standard hiring?
- Does the "3 days in the office" change seasonally or by team, and is it enforced?
- Are there any restrictions on working from outside Denmark (for example, occasional remote work from the Netherlands)?
- How does the company handle the notice period and the start date if things slip — is 2027-02-01 firm?

## Trade-offs

By accepting:

- Gains: the start date (2027-02-01) matches your earliest possible start, and they accept your three months notice. Copenhagen is your ideal location. The on-site pattern of 3 days is within your limit. Base pay is stated as DKK 720,000 per year, with a pension of 12 percent and 6 weeks holiday. Equity is explicitly none, so the pay is all cash apart from the bonus.
- Gives up: no equity participation while you stay. The "up to 10 percent" bonus is capped and conditional in ways the offer does not specify, so total pay is not fixed. Some terms you care about — hours, on-call and compensation for it, the team and level, and progression toward tech lead — are not stated, so accepting means accepting these as defined by the employment contract rather than by this offer. You would take on the uncertainty of relocation from the Netherlands with no stated support, and an office location you cannot yet check against your one-hour commute limit.
- Risks: if the role involves uncompensated on-call, that would meet one of your deal-breakers after you have committed. If the role turns out to be a broad generalist position rather than an individual contributor or tech-lead role on a data platform team, it would not match your stated preference.

By declining:

- Gains: you stay in your current employed position and keep the option to pursue an individual-contributor or tech-lead role on a data platform team with clearer terms, or with equity, elsewhere. You avoid moving country (from the Netherlands to Denmark) for terms that are only partly specified.
- Gives up: a senior data engineer role in Copenhagen, your ideal location, with a start date that aligns with your notice period. You would forfeit the stated DKK 720,000 per year base, the up-to-10-percent bonus, the 12 percent pension and the 6 weeks holiday, and you would need another route (or another offer) to reach Copenhagen. Your decision to leave your current employer would no longer be driven by this offer.
- Risks: other opportunities may have later or less convenient start dates, or may not be in Copenhagen at all. Without benchmark data supplied for this comparison, you cannot yet tell whether the stated pay is favourable.

Your decision.
