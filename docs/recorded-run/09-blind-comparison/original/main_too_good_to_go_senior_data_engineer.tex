\vspace{6pt}
\small{Data engineer with seven years in retail and logistics, building batch and streaming pipelines for operational supply-chain data and the warehouses they feed. I own a 40-million-events-per-day shipment pipeline end to end, from Kafka through Airflow into PostgreSQL and BigQuery. I have cut rebuild times, warehouse costs and failed loads with dbt, partitioning and data-quality tests. I want to own a pipeline inside a product team, where logistics data shapes the product and the pipeline owner sits with the people who use it.}
