# Verified company research

- Falck describes itself as a global market leader in emergency response and healthcare services. (source: https://www.falck.com/)
- Falck has 25 countries, more than 25,000 employees and has delivered 9.2 million emergency response and healthcare services. (source: https://www.falck.com/)
- Falck's stated mission is to help people feel safe and live healthier lives. (source: https://www.falck.com/)
- Falck's self-described purpose is to advance sustainable healthcare to meet people's changing needs. (source: https://www.falck.com/about-us/who-are-we/)
- Falck names Care, Reliable and Advance as its stated values. (source: https://www.falck.com/about-us/who-are-we/)
- Falck's business areas cover healthcare subscriptions, travel assistance, employee and labour market services, Latin American home healthcare, ambulance services and fire services. (source: https://www.falck.com/about-us/who-are-we/business-areas/)
- Falck operates more than 3,000 ambulances in 11 countries. (source: https://www.falck.com/about-us/who-are-we/business-areas/)
- Falck's ambulance business reports 1.7 million ambulance services per year and 13,000 colleagues. (source: https://www.falck.com/services/healthcare/ambulance/)
- Falck's culture rests on three 'Winning Behaviours'. (source: https://www.falck.com/careers/working-at-falck/our-culture/)
- One of Falck's Winning Behaviours requires transparency and high compliance standards. (source: https://www.falck.com/careers/working-at-falck/our-culture/)
- Falck emphasizes cross-functional, cross-country collaboration. (source: https://www.falck.com/careers/working-at-falck/our-culture/)
- Falck's personnel culture award has been given to an AI manager, which shows that AI work is recognised. (source: https://www.falck.com/careers/working-at-falck/our-culture/)
- Falck frames its innovation around three Solution Spaces: Ease Critical Moments, Enable Equal Access, and Build Resilient Communities. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/innovation-approach/)
- Project Lighthouse was piloted across 11 ambulance stations in Region Zealand over 10 weeks in 2023. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/innovation-approach/project-lighthouse/)
- In 2025 Falck had revenue of DKK 12,495 million and EBIT of DKK 505 million, and it raised its 2026 EBIT expectations. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/annual-report-falck-raises-earnings-expectations-for-2026/)
- In Q4 2025 Falck restructured into four regions: Scandinavia, Europe, the US and Latin America. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/annual-report-falck-raises-earnings-expectations-for-2026/)
- The regional restructuring was followed by changes at other levels to cut costs. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/annual-report-falck-raises-earnings-expectations-for-2026/)
- Falck now runs ambulance services in Boulder, Colorado, in the US, and its Alameda contract was extended. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/annual-report-falck-raises-earnings-expectations-for-2026/)
- In April 2026 Johanna Rastad became director of the new Region Scandinavia, and Jørgen Mieritz became director of Region Europe. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/new-directors-for-scandinavia-and-europe-join-falcks-group-executive-management/)
- Lars Dam Jensen, former Region Europe director, and Sonja Karaoglan, interim Scandinavia director, left the Group Executive Management. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/new-directors-for-scandinavia-and-europe-join-falcks-group-executive-management/)
- The new Europe director's focus is stable operations, and a key challenge is starting large ambulance contracts in Catalonia and Skania. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/new-directors-for-scandinavia-and-europe-join-falcks-group-executive-management/)
- Region Europe covers ambulance contracts in Denmark, Sweden, Germany and Spain, plus the fire services business. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/new-directors-for-scandinavia-and-europe-join-falcks-group-executive-management/)
- In June 2026, Catalina Giraldo became Head of Region Latam and joined Group Executive Management. She replaced Gorka Vigara, who left Falck. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/falck-appoints-catalina-giraldo-head-of-region-latam/)
- Falck's Latam region delivers about 2.5 million healthcare services a year and operates in Colombia, Uruguay, El Salvador, Panama and Ecuador. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/falck-appoints-catalina-giraldo-head-of-region-latam/)
- Jakob Riis is CEO and President of Falck. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/falck-appoints-catalina-giraldo-head-of-region-latam/)
- In March 2026, Peter Bason (KIRKBI) joined Falck's Board of Directors, replacing Thomas Schleicher. Dorthe Mikkelsen also left. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/peter-bason-joins-falcks-board-of-directors/)
- Niels Smedegaard chairs Falck's Board of Directors. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/peter-bason-joins-falcks-board-of-directors/)
- In June 2026, Falck formed a global travel-security partnership with Control Risks. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/falck-and-control-risks-enter-a-strategic-partnership-for-global-travel-security/)
- In January 2026, Falck won the Skåne 2 ambulance district in Sweden, effective 1 October 2026. (source: https://www.falck.com/news/latest-news/falck-is-awarded-ambulance-contract-in-skane-sweden/)
- Falck states that it has been a participant in the UN Global Compact since 2019. (source: https://www.falck.com/about-us/sustainability/)
